Have handy: your insurance card (for the superbill), a list of current medications and dosages, and the name and contact information of your primary care provider.

4 · Notice of Privacy Practices & Acknowledgement

Notice of Privacy Practices

The Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”) is a federal program that requires that all medical records and other individually identifiable health information used or disclosed by us in any form — whether electronically, on paper, or orally — are kept properly confidential.

Permitted uses & disclosures

  • Treatment — providing, coordinating, or managing your health care.
  • Payment — reimbursement, billing, collection, and utilization review.
  • Health care operations — quality assessment, auditing, cost management, and customer service.
  • De-identified health information.
  • Appointment reminders and health-related information.
  • Any other use requires your written authorization, which you may revoke in writing.

Your rights

  • Request restrictions on uses and disclosures, including to family or friends.
  • Request confidential communications by alternative means or at alternative locations.
  • Inspect and copy your protected health information.
  • Amend your protected health information.
  • Receive an accounting of disclosures.
  • Obtain a paper copy of this notice.

Effective date & changes

This notice is effective January 1, 2019. The practice reserves the right to change the terms of this notice and to make the new provisions effective for all information it maintains.

Complaints

You have the right to file a written complaint with our office, or with the Department of Health & Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, about violations of the provisions of this notice: U.S. Dept. of Health & Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, 200 Independence Avenue S.W., Washington, D.C. 20201.

Patient acknowledgement

I acknowledge that:

  • my health information is private and confidential;
  • the practice works to protect that privacy;
  • my information may be used for treatment, billing, and health care operations;
  • in unusual circumstances the law may require release without my permission;
  • the full Notice of Privacy Practices contains the complete policies and my rights, including access, restrictions, an accounting of disclosures, and communication preferences;
  • the practice has established procedures for exercising those rights.
